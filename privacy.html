Privacy

Prism Atoll keeps your game on your device.

The Vision Pro app

Prism Atoll saves your game and audio preference locally. The app has no account, advertising, analytics or developer-operated game server. It uses system gestures to interact with the board.

This website

The marketing pages and browser demo include no analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages may process technical information needed to deliver the site.

Support messages

The support page links to the developer’s contact form on a separate site. If you use it, Web3Forms processes the contact details and message you submit so the developer can respond. Use the same contact route for questions about your data.
