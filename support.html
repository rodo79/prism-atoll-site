Common questions

How do I turn and drop a pair?

Look at the floating pair and pinch once to turn it or twice to drop it. You can also use Rotate and Drop below the board. If a landing ring is red, choose another column or turn the pair.

Why didn’t four marbles bloom?

At least four marbles of the same color must connect through shared faces. Connections can turn corners and go up or down levels. Diagonals don’t count.

Can I change modes mid-game?

Use Menu below the board, then choose Normal Mode or Full Immersion. Your board, score and upcoming pairs are saved on your Vision Pro. Start over begins a new game.

Can I mute the music?

Use the speaker button below the board. Music also pauses when you return to the menu.

Contact the developer

Mention Prism Atoll in the developer’s support form. For a bug, include the app and visionOS versions, the game mode and steps to reproduce it.
