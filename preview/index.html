Demo rules

Each pair contains two different colors. The marbles fall into their own columns and stop on top of the stacks. Groups of at least four matching marbles that share faces bloom and disappear. The marbles above can fall into new groups and trigger a cascade. Diagonals don’t count. The board has 25 columns across a 5 × 5 grid and three levels.

The game ends when no pair fits. Hover over the board to choose a position, then click to turn the pair. Symbols distinguish the colors. The timer pauses when you switch away from the page. Each new game uses the same sequence so you can try different strategies.

This demo shows the puzzle with browser controls. On Apple Vision Pro, the app has a spatial board, two-handed gestures and an immersive garden.
